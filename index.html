THE PROBLEM

Too many opportunities. Not enough guidance.

Girls interested in science, coding, engineering and technology often struggle to find reliable information about where to start. Opportunities can be scattered across websites, social media and different organizations.

STEAM Locator Nigeria brings these resources together in one easy-to-use platform.
