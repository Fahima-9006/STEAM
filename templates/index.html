WHY STEAM LOCATOR?

Finding your next opportunity should not be difficult.

Opportunities can be difficult to find when information is spread across different websites, organizations and social media platforms.

STEAM Locator Nigeria brings useful opportunities together so students can spend less time searching and more time learning, building and growing.
